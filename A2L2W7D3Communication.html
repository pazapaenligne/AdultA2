Communication Mission

A delay and a change of plan

Mariam's train is delayed. She must call Hana, say sorry, and explain her new plan.

Your mission: tell Hana about the delay, answer her follow-up question, then make your message better.

1 · Prepare

Words for delays

  • delayed
  • cancelled
  • sandstorm
  • traffic jam
  • alternative
  • next available
  • reschedule
  • apologise

2 · Rehearse

Try the phrases

  • The flight will probably be delayed.
  • There's a big traffic jam on the highway.
  • I'll take the metro instead.

Choose how you want to practise.

Say your sentence out loud. Then check:

3 · First attempt

Call Hana

Hana
Hello Mariam. Are you on your way?

Mariam's facts: the train is delayed · she won't reach the station at 8:30 · she'll take a taxi instead · she'll arrive at the office at 9:15 · the discussion can start at 9:20.

Say sorry, explain the delay, give your new plan, and ask Hana to let you know.
