Tradition comparison note
Fictional practice document
At home, Mariam’s family used to celebrate with a large meal at her grandmother’s house. Her sister used to prepare a special dish. Now the family lives in different cities, so they often call each other. Mariam still cooks that dish and enjoys sharing it with friends. She says both ways feel special.

Your question

What is this text mainly helping the reader understand or do?

Model purpose

The note compares how Mariam’s family used to celebrate with how the family stays in touch now. It shows how to describe a past tradition.

Self-check: Did I mention the past tradition? Did I mention what is different now?
