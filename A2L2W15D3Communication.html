  • Purpose
    Say why you are writing.
  • Detail
    Say what, where and when.
  • Interaction
    Ask for a reply or a next step.
  • Close
    Thank them. Add your name.

To: Building Management

Dear Building Management,

I would like to make a formal complaint about the noise from Flat 6 after 10 p.m. I spoke to Sara on 3 May. The noise happened again on 5 May. Could you reply in writing by Friday 8 May? If there is no reply, I will contact the landlord.

Thank you for your help.
Kind regards,
Mariam, Flat 4
