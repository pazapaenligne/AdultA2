    Word help (optional):
    metro
    a train service within a city
    fare
    the price of a journey
    ride app
    an app used to book a car journey

    What is this text mainly helping the reader understand or do?

    A model answer

    It helps the reader understand Sara's journey plan: when and where she will meet Hana, and what she will do if the metro is delayed.

      Sara is going to take the metro on Monday. She plans to leave home at eight and meet Hana at Central Station at eight thirty. They will walk to the office together. If the metro is delayed, Sara will send Hana a message. The office meeting begins at nine.

      A model reply

      I'm going to take the metro on Monday. I'm going to meet Hana at Central Station at eight thirty. If the metro is delayed, I'll send her a message.

      Your reply can be different. The plan does not say whether Hana will answer the message.
