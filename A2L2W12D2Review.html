    Find a detail

    Mariam is comparing two schools. Answer first, without looking at the text.

    Which school is cheaper?

    How did you get your answer?

    The text

    School A is nearer to Mariam's home and has a school bus. Its sample fee is 600 units. School B costs 550 units, but the journey is longer and there is a waiting list. Mariam wants a short journey and an available place. Both schools offer a visit by appointment.

    The highlighted words give the answer: 550 is less than 600.

    Repair one sentence

    Mariam wrote this about two schools. Tap the word that does not belong.

    School A is more nearer.
    School A is nearer.

    Nearer already means "more near". Use one, not both.

    Reply, then improve

    A colleague asks: “Which school suits your needs?”
    A possible reply: School A is nearer. Is there an available place?
    Different words are fine if your meaning is clear.
