Look at the situation

Two learners meet at the end of a course. Look at the picture. What can you see?

Hana and Mariam are in a learning centre. Hana has finished her A2 course. They are talking about her progress and her next steps.

What do you think they are talking about? Choose any ideas you like.

In this lesson you will hear a short conversation about progress, goals and what you are going to do next.

Listen to the conversation

Listen once or many times. You can show the text if you want to.

Mariam
Hana, you finished A2! What is your biggest achievement?
Hana
I can talk to my neighbours in English now. That is my achievement.
Mariam
That is great progress! What is your goal now?
Hana
My goal is B1. I'm going to practise every day.
Mariam
And what are you going to do first?
Hana
I'm going to make a plan. I'm going to continue my lessons on Mondays.

Explore a progress journey

A journey has a beginning, a middle and a next step. Open each card.

Where I started

"A year ago, I could not talk on the phone in English."

Where I am now

"My achievement is simple: I can make an appointment by phone."

Where I want to go

"My goal is B1. I'm going to study for it."

Next steps can be different. Look at three people. You do not need to choose.

Work

"I'm going to ask for a new job at the centre."

Community

"I'm going to join a conversation group on Fridays."

Study

"I'm going to study for 20 minutes every day."

Notice useful language

Tap the underlined words. See what they mean.

Mariam
That is great ! What is your now?
Hana
That is my . practise every day.
progress
You are better than before.
goal
Something you want to do in the future.
achievement
Something good you did.
I'm going to + verb
A plan for the future. "I'm going to study on Monday."

Where could this happen?

You might talk about progress and next steps in many places. Tap any ideas that fit your life.
