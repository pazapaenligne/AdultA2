Your mission: What do I need to do?

Mariam has an appointment at a service office. She wants to know what she has to do. You are Mariam. Talk with a desk assistant and find out what you need to bring and what you have to do.

Your job: ask what you need to bring, ask what you have to do first, ask one more question, and close politely.
