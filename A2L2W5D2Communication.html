Communication Mission

The tenancy contract

Your mission

Mariam is thinking about renting the Metro Apartment. Hana, the agent, has sent her the contract to review. You are Mariam.

Message from Hana: “Dear Mariam, here is the contract for review. Please read the notice clause before signing. If you have a question, send it to me by Thursday.”

Contract facts
HomeMetro Apartment, two bedrooms, unfurnished
Term15 July 2026 – 14 July 2027
Rent48,000 AED a year, four cheques of 12,000 AED
Deposit2,400 AED, before you collect the keys
Agent fee1,000 AED
NoticeIn writing, 60 days

What you will do

  1. Ask Hana about two contract details.
  2. Listen to her answers and reply to what she says.
  3. Say what you will do if you decide to sign.
  4. Check your reply, improve one thing, and try again.

This is a practice contract. All the names and numbers are fictional.

Useful phrases

  • How many cheques do you accept?
  • Is the deposit refundable?
  • Do I have to register the contract?
  • If I decide to sign, I’ll …
