Pazapa A2 · Week 14 · Lesson 1 · Communication Mission

What happened?

Step 1 of 7

Your mission

You are Mariam. There was a small accident near the car park at work. Hana is a colleague and she wants to know what happened. Tell her what you were doing and what you saw.

Useful facts
  • You were walking near the car park when you heard a loud noise.
  • A car hit a low barrier. The driver stopped immediately.
  • Nobody was injured. Security wrote down the place and time.

You can add your own fictional details.

Your reply should
  • Purpose: say what you were doing and what happened.
  • Detail: add one useful detail.
  • Interaction: answer Hana’s question.
  • Close: say what happens next, or finish politely.

Step 2 of 7

Look at an example

This is a worked example. It is one possible conversation, not a real one.

Hana

Hi Mariam. I heard there was an accident near the car park. What were you doing when it happened?

Mariam

I was walking near the car park when I heard a loud noise.

Hana

Oh. What did you see?

Mariam

A car hit a low barrier. It happened suddenly. The driver stopped immediately and nobody was injured.

Hana

Good. Thank you for telling me.

Mariam

You’re welcome. I told the security assistant too.

Notice

was walking (longer action) + when + heard (short event).
