Pazapa English A2 · Week 14 · Lesson 3 · Communication Mission

Heat, sun and staying safe

    Your mission

    Help your neighbour Hana. Tell her about the courtyard notice, say where to go and explain one instruction.

    You are Mariam. You can use your own ideas too.

    Practice notice

    Courtyard closed

    • The courtyard is closed this afternoon during maintenance after strong winds.
    • Residents must use the indoor entrance.
    • They mustn't enter the marked work area.
    • Building staff will post an update at five.
    • For access, contact reception.
    Do this well

    Say what you need or want · add one useful detail · answer Hana's question · end politely or say the next step.

    Worked example

    Example only A different situation. This is not a real conversation.

    SaraHi Mariam. Can you help me with the pool notice?
    MariamOf course. You mustn't leave children in the car.
    SaraAnd what about the sun?
    MariamAvoid the sun between eleven and three. You must drink water every hour outside.
    SaraThanks, that's clear.
