Your mission: ask for time off

Mission

Ask for a day off, offer a cover plan and check when you will get a reply.

Facts to use

  • Sara would like Friday off. Mariam can cover.
  • Hana needs the dates in writing. She will reply tomorrow.
  • The request is not approved yet.

You can be yourself or use the fictional role. Use fictional details only.

You did it well if you:

  1. Say what you need (purpose).
  2. Give one useful, accurate detail.
  3. Answer or ask a follow-up question.
  4. Confirm a next step or end politely.

Worked example

Worked example, written for this lesson. Not a real recording.
Sara (asking)Could I take Friday off?
HanaThank you for asking in advance. How will you arrange cover?
SaraMariam can help. I'll send the request in writing today.
HanaThank you. I need to check the team schedule. I'll reply tomorrow.
Useful phrases (optional)
  • Could I take next Thursday off?
  • I'd like to request two weeks' leave in August.
  • Would it be possible to leave early today?
