    Back in the team meeting

    Mariam is at her first team meeting. Read how Hana opens it.

    HanaWhat's on the agenda today?
    SaraThe welcome email for Monday's visitors.
    MariamI'd like to make a suggestion.

    agenda = the topics to discuss in a meeting. suggestion = an idea for other people to consider.
