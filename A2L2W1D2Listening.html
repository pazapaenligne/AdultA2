Listening Mission

Amina tells her friend Sara about the day she arrived. Listen and find out what happened. Amina raconte à son amie Sara le jour de son arrivée. Écoutez et découvrez ce qui s’est passé.

Listen to the conversation.

Listen again for the details.

Check what you heard.

    Amina’s arrival

    You followed Amina’s two days. Vous avez suivi les deux jours d’Amina.

    Arrival night

    • Amina landed.
    • She took a taxi to the hotel.
    • She checked in.

    Next morning
