Changing a service

Listen to a customer and an adviser. Find out what the customer finally asks for. Écoutez une cliente et une conseillère. Trouvez ce que la cliente demande à la fin.

Words you may hear

  • cancel
  • upgrade
  • fee
  • reference number

You can listen as many times as you like. Vous pouvez écouter autant de fois que vous voulez.

Listen for the main idea

What does the customer finally request? Que demande finalement la cliente ?

Listen for the details

Listen again. Choose one answer for each question. Écoutez encore. Choisissez une réponse pour chaque question.

Improve your answers

What you heard

  • The customer thought about cancelling. Then she asked for an upgrade.

  • The change takes two working days.

  • Her reference number is R82.
