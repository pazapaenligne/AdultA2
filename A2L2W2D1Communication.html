Communication Mission: Have you ever…?

Meet Mariam

Mariam is a new colleague. She wants to know about life here.

  1. 1Ask Mariam about two experiences.
  2. 2Answer her question.
  3. 3Add one past detail.
Worked example
M
MariamHello! I’m Mariam. I’m new here. Have you ever been to a souk?
You
YouYes, I have. I went last Friday.
M
MariamWas it the old souk?
You
YouYes, it was. Have you ever been to the desert?
M
MariamNo, I haven’t. Let’s go together next time!

Prepare your reply

Choose two experiences. You will ask Mariam about them.

Your mission

Ask Mariam about two experiences. Answer her question. Add one past detail.

Two useful facts

  • Mariam went to the old souk last Friday with her sister.
  • She bought some dates, but she hasn’t tried the local bread yet.

A good message

  • It asks about two experiences.
  • It has one detail about the past.
  • It answers Mariam’s question.
  • It ends politely or chooses a day.

Useful phrases

Have you ever been to the desert? I’ve never tried camel milk. I’ve been to Dubai, but I haven’t been to Oman.
