Week 14 · Lesson 2 · Communication Mission

Calling for help

Your mission

Mission: practise a help call. Say where you are, say what happened, and answer one question.

  • PlaceThe main entrance of the sample building, beside the blue sign.
  • What happenedSomeone fell near the steps. Another person is with her.
  • You areMariam. You speak to Hana.

A worked example

MariamI need an ambulance. Someone fell near the steps.
HanaWhich entrance are you near?
MariamI'm near the main entrance, beside the blue sign.
HanaStay on the line while I check the location.
MariamThank you. I will stay on the line.

This is a worked example, not a real call.

Useful phrases

  • I need an ambulance.
  • There's been an accident on the highway near the [landmark].
  • Two people are hurt. One is not conscious.

Words to use: emergency, ambulance, police, fire, location, landmark, conscious, breathing, stay on the line.

How you will check your work

  • Purpose
    Did I say what I need?
  • Detail
    Did I give one useful detail?
  • Interaction
    Did I answer a question?
  • Close
    Did I end politely or say a next step?

This is language practice. It is not emergency guidance. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number. All details here are fictional.
