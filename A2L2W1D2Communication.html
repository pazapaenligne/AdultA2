Communication Mission

The day I arrived

Sara is your new colleague. Give her a short voice note about your arrival.

Your mission

Tell Sara three things that happened, in order. Your travel details can be fictional.

You are ready when you can:
  • say why you are telling Sara
  • add one useful detail
  • answer a question from Sara
  • finish politely

See how it sounds

Mariam talks to Sara. Notice the past forms: arrived, took, was.

Worked example

This is one possible conversation. Your story can be different.

MariamHi Sara. I arrived on Tuesday. The flight landed at nine in the evening.
SaraHow did you get to the hotel?
MariamI took a taxi to the hotel.
SaraWas your luggage OK?
MariamYes, it arrived with me, but I was very tired. Thank you. See you tomorrow.
