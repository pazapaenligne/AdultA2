A misunderstanding: two views

Open each view. Nobody is wrong. Then see the next step they share.

Employee view

“I worked extra hours. I would like to explain what happened.”

Manager view

“I need to see the hours record. I will check with HR.”

The next step they share

  1. Mariam sends her hours record.
  2. Hana checks with HR.
  3. They resolve it together.
