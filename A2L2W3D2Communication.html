Communication Mission

Your mission

Mariam has a medical test on Wednesday. Her friend Hana needs help. Explain three steps to Hana. Then check one detail with the assistant.

Practice slip
  • Appointment: Wednesday, 9:30
  • Ticket: 32
  • Check in at the front desk first.
  • Wait near counter four.
  • When your number appears, go to the counter.
  • After the appointment, ask reception where to find your result message.
You can do it well if you…
  • Purpose: say what you need or want.
  • Detail: give one useful and correct detail.
  • Interaction: answer or ask a question.
  • Close: say the next step or end politely.

You can speak as Mariam or as yourself.

See an example

Read how Mariam helps Hana.

Worked example

HanaMariam, can you help me with my appointment?
MariamYes, of course.
HanaWhat should I do first?
MariamFirst, check in at the front desk. Then wait near counter four.
HanaWhich counter do I go to?
MariamCounter four. When your number appears, go to the counter.
HanaThank you!
MariamYou're welcome. See you there.
Useful phrases
  • I'm here for the medical test.
  • Where do I get a ticket number?
  • Which counter do I go to?
  • First, … Then, … After that, … Finally, …
