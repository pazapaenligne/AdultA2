Open the portal

Tap each message in the notification drawer to open it.

“Leila’s Term 1 report is ready in the portal.”

A notification is a short message that tells you something new is ready.

“Attendance: 90%. That is 18 of 20 days.”

A report shows how a student is doing at school: grades, attendance and comments.

“Effort is good. Please practise multiplication for 10 minutes, 4 days a week.”

Effort is how hard a student tries.

“Please confirm by 27 November.”

A parent-teacher meeting is a time to talk about the report with the teacher.
