    Communication mission

    What the doctor told me

    Your friend Hana asks about your clinic visit. Tell her what the doctor told you, then answer one question.

    Your mission

    Tell Hana what the doctor told you, add one useful detail and say your next step.

    • You went to the clinic this morning.
    • The doctor told you to rest, and you need a follow-up appointment.
    • You have not booked it yet. The reception message says you should call tomorrow to ask for a time.

    You will do well if you:

    • Explain the purpose
    • Give one useful detail
    • Answer a follow-up
    • Finish with a next step

    This is private practice with a fictional story. You do not need to give real details.

    Prepare

    Read how the language works

    Worked example — written for practice, not a recording
    HanaHi Mariam. How was the clinic?
    MariamHi Hana. I went to the clinic this morning.
    HanaWhat did the doctor tell you?
    MariamShe told me to rest for two days. She said I need more water.
    HanaOK. Do you have another appointment?
    MariamYes. I have to go back on Thursday at 10:30. Thanks, Hana!

    Notice the pattern

    told me to + action: “The doctor told me to rest.”
    said + sentence: “She said I need more water.”

    When you report, you becomes I and your becomes my.

    Phrase bank

    • The doctor told me to rest for two days.
    • She said I need more water.
    • He said the results were fine.

    Use these as help. You can use your own words.
