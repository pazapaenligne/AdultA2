    Review, improve and retry

    What happened?

    In this lesson you told a short story about an accident. Now tell it again, a little better.

    Try each situation from memory first. Use a hint if you need one. Needing a hint is normal.

    Look at all three situations to carry on.

    Check the detail

    Answer from memory. Then look at the text and check your answer.

    The short text

    Sara was walking near the car park when she heard a loud noise. A car had hit a low barrier. The driver stopped immediately. Sara stayed at a safe distance and told the security assistant what she saw. Nobody in this fictional scene was injured. Security wrote down the location and time.

    The text is covered. Try the question first.

    How did you answer?

    Say it

    Written model. Read it, then say it aloud.

    I was driving to work when a car hit us.

    Written recall

    A written question about the story. This is not a listening test.

    Which road was the accident on?

    Answer the question and check the text to carry on.
