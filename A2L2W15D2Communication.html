A small disagreement

Your mission: make a polite request to a neighbour, give a reason and agree a fair solution.

Choose your situation

Your situation

    You succeed when you:

    • say what you need (purpose)
    • give one useful detail
    • answer your neighbour
    • agree a next step or finish politely
    Useful words and phrases
    • I understand, but the noise is very late at night.
    • Could you keep it down after ten?
    • Someone is using our parking space.
    • noise
    • late at night
    • parking
    • compromise
    • fair
    • apologise
