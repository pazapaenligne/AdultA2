      Dear Mariam,

      here is the sample contract for review. The deposit in this exercise is 500 units.

      Please read the notice clause before signing.

      If you have a question, send it to me by Thursday. We can arrange a second viewing on Friday.

      This example does not describe a real tenancy agreement.

      Repair one point

      Draft message

      If I sign, I payed the deposit tomorrow.

      My better sentence

      If I sign, I'll pay the deposit tomorrow.

      One useful point: After “If I sign,” talk about tomorrow with will + an action: I'll pay.

      Think of the plan. Tomorrow has not happened yet.
