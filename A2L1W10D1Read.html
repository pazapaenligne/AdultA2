    Mariam has worked in customer service for four years. Last year she worked at a housing office. She answered questions, checked documents and arranged appointments. She has used booking software, but she hasn't managed a team. She is applying for a reception position and can provide a reference on request.

    Word help
    apply
    ask to be considered for a job
    position
    a job or role in an organisation
    reference
    a person who can recommend you for a job

    A possible answer

    It summarises Mariam’s work experience so a reader can think about her for a reception position.
