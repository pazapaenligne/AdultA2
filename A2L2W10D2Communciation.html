Your mission

You are in an interview for a reception job. Answer Hana’s questions about your experience, a strength and your availability. Then ask one question about the role.

Your task panel
  • The interview is on Wednesday at ten.
  • Prepare one example of helping a customer.
  • Reply by Tuesday to confirm.
Success criteria
  • Purpose: say what you want or need.
  • Detail: add one useful detail.
  • Interaction: answer or ask a follow-up.
  • Close: confirm a next step or end politely.

You can use your own details or make them up. Please do not share real documents or numbers. This is private practice. Nothing is sent anywhere.
