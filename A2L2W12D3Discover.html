Hear the situation

Press Listen to hear the conversation. You can listen again, or read it when you want.

Parent
Hello. How is Leila settling in?
Teacher
She is settling in well. Maths is a concern, but she can improve with support.
Parent
What can we do at home?
Teacher
Practise ten minutes after dinner, Monday to Thursday. Next steps: check the portal together. I will send a practice pack.

Follow the conversation

Tap each picture to see what happens next.

The teacher says there is a concern: “Maths is a concern.” She is kind and clear.
The teacher offers support: “She can improve with support.” Then she says what to do at home.
They agree next steps: practise at home, check the portal together, and use the practice pack.
Model summary: Leila is settling in well. Maths is a concern. Practise ten minutes after dinner, Monday to Thursday, and check the portal together. The teacher sends a practice pack, and they meet again next term.
